How to choose between these two

3 · Bauhaus wins when…

  • You want one visual language that teaches the product (gap / root / lesson = circle / triangle / square).
  • Admin + corporate screens must feel serious and scannable for ops and B2B buyers.
  • Any-subject expansion should still feel like a system, not a kids’ app.
  • Pitch / investor decks need intellectual credibility.

7 · Playful wins when…

  • Retention and streaks are the growth lever (already in the product).
  • Schools, nonprofits, and individual learners are the primary wedge.
  • You want the brand to feel warm and sticky on first open.
  • You’re OK dialing playfulness down on admin (or using a quieter admin variant).
Hybrid worth considering: Bauhaus for marketing + admin + corporate, with Playful accents only on learner streaks/XP/checkpoints. Tell me 3, 7, or hybrid and I’ll rebuild the real site + start aligning app tokens.
